Lucia & Jason busts in meme.png (v3.3): original flat-vector
illustrations drawn from scratch in code (generate_meme.py,
draw_characters — Pillow primitives only, palette pink #D1006B /
ink / paper + skin tints). NOT copied or traced from any
official Rockstar artwork, screenshot, or key art. The likeness
was built by STUDYING official references (Rockstar's Lucia
portrait, Jason hero render, duo key art — viewed as reference
only, files never copied into this repo) and rebuilding each
character's signature silhouette in code: Lucia's high ponytail
with a wavy cascade, stern angled brows, hoop earrings and chain;
Jason's short sandy-blonde faded cut, visible eyes, light stubble
band on a squared jaw. Small pink circle between them is an
original Vice City sun accent.
